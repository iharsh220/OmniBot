OmniChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

OmniChat (omnibot-phi.vercel.app) is a free tool for live streamers: chat and stats overlays, alerts, polls, chat bots, Discord announcements and a UPI donation page. This policy explains what OmniChat collects, why, how long it is kept and how to remove it. Questions: omnichat220@gmail.com.

1. Google and YouTube data

OmniChat uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

OmniChat's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train artificial intelligence models. It is passed on only to the service providers listed in section 8, to places you choose yourself (for example your overlay or your Discord server), or when the law requires it.

2. Kick data

If you connect Kick, OmniChat reads your Kick profile and channel (name, picture, ID, live status, viewers, title, category, subscribers) and receives chat messages, follows and subscriptions of your channel from Kick. Kick's sign-in also asks for the permission to write chat messages. This permission is used only for OmniChat's own Kick bot account; OmniChat does not post from a streamer's Kick account. Your follower count is read by your own browser from kick.com while the dashboard is open.

3. Other data you give or create

  • Settings and widgets: overlay designs, bot commands, timers, polls, alert texts.
  • Uploads: alert pictures and sounds. They are served at a public, hard-to-guess address so your streaming app can load them.
  • Donation page: your UPI ID, payee name and page text, which are public on your donation page. When a viewer sends a note, OmniChat keeps the name, amount and message they typed. Payments go directly between the viewer's and your UPI app: OmniChat never handles money and never sees bank or card details.
  • Discord (optional): if you add OmniChat to your server, OmniChat stores the server's ID and name and your announcement settings, reads the channel list and the bot's permissions, and posts announcements there. Your other messages are not read for this.
  • Viewers: names, public profile pictures and messages of people who write in your live chat, poll votes, and Super Chat, membership, follow and subscription events, as delivered by YouTube and Kick.

4. Security and activity log

To keep accounts safe, find problems and help streamers who ask for support, OmniChat records:

  • Sign-ins, sign-outs and failed sign-ins, with date and time
  • What you do in the dashboard (for example: widget edited, Egg Bot turned on, page opened) and Discord announcements
  • Your IP address, approximate location derived from it (city, region, country) and browser / device type
  • Errors that happen while you use OmniChat

Only the OmniChat administrator can see this log, together with your channel names, connection and live status, widget names, Discord server name and system logs, and uses it only for security, support and fixing problems. It does not contain passwords, OAuth tokens, saved chat or payment details. IP addresses and devices of your viewers (overlays, donation page) are not added to it; system logs can contain the name of a viewer who used a bot command.

5. Help and support

When you use Help & support, your message, the e-mail address you enter (optional), your screenshots, and your IP address, approximate location and device are saved for the administrator, and the message with screenshots is sent to the OmniChat support mailbox. Please hide private details in screenshots. E-mails already in the mailbox can be removed on request.

6. Cookies and browser storage

OmniChat uses only cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in (30 days, extended while you use OmniChat), short-lived ones that protect the sign-in (10 minutes) and one that remembers your last login for “Continue as” (up to 1 year, removable on the login page). Your browser also stores small preferences such as the theme. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Service providers

  • Google / YouTube and Kick: sign-in, channel and chat data
  • Vercel: hosting; also provides the approximate location of an IP address
  • Upstash (Redis): data storage
  • Discord: announcements, if you connect a server
  • Gmail: delivers support requests to our mailbox
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google, which receives your IP address for this. Channel pictures and thumbnails are loaded from YouTube, Kick and Discord in the same way.

These providers may process data outside your country. OmniChat does not use third-party analytics or advertising and does not sell personal data.

9. Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored, sessions are stored only as hashes, connections use HTTPS, and OmniChat never sees your Google or Kick password. No system is perfectly secure; if you think your account is at risk, sign out everywhere in Settings and write to us.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect a platform: dashboard → Connections → Disconnect. OmniChat revokes its access and deletes the tokens.
  • Remove access at the provider: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; Kick in your Kick account settings; Discord by removing the bot from your server.
  • Clear or limit chat history, clear logs, sign out everywhere: dashboard → Settings and Logs.
  • Delete everything: dashboard → Settings → Delete account. This revokes access and removes your account and the data linked to it from OmniChat's storage. Messages already posted in chats or on Discord, and e-mails already sent, stay where they are.
  • Copy, correction or removal of your data: write to us. This also works for viewers whose messages or donation notes were saved. We may ask you to show that the account is yours.

11. Children

OmniChat is meant for people who are old enough to have their own YouTube or Kick account under the rules of those platforms and the law of their country. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; write to us and we will remove it.

12. Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Important changes are announced in the dashboard or on the login page.

13. Contact
