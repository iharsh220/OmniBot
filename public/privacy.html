OmniChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What data we access

OmniChat connects to your YouTube and/or Kick accounts via OAuth to read:

  • Live chat messages (for display in your OBS overlay)
  • Channel info (name, avatar, subscriber/follower count)
  • Stream status (live/offline)

How we use your data

  • Display chat messages and stats in your browser widget (for OBS capture)
  • Store OAuth tokens securely (encrypted) to maintain your connection
  • Never share, sell, or use your data for advertising

Security and activity log

To keep accounts safe, find problems and help streamers who ask for support, OmniChat records:

  • Sign-ins, sign-outs and failed sign-ins, with date and time
  • What you do in the dashboard (for example: widget edited, Egg Bot turned on, page opened)
  • Your IP address, approximate location derived from it (city, region, country) and browser / device type
  • Errors that happen while you use OmniChat

Only the OmniChat administrator can see this log, together with your channel names, connection and live status, widget names and system logs. It does not contain passwords, OAuth tokens, chat messages or payment details. Entries are kept for 90 days and removed when you delete your account. IP addresses and devices of your viewers (overlays, donation page) are not recorded; system logs can contain the name of a viewer who used a bot command.

Help and support

When you use Help & support, your message, the e-mail address you enter (optional), your screenshots, and your IP address, approximate location and device are saved for the OmniChat administrator and the message with screenshots is sent to the OmniChat support mailbox. Please hide private details in screenshots. Saved requests are deleted after 90 days or when you delete your account; e-mails already in the support mailbox can be removed on request.

Discord

If you add OmniChat to your Discord server, OmniChat stores the server's ID and name and your announcement settings, reads the channel list and the bot's permissions, and posts announcements there. Nothing else from your server is read for this. Disconnecting or deleting your account removes it.

Data storage

Data is stored in Upstash Redis (connected through Vercel). OAuth tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored. You can delete your account anytime from the dashboard, which permanently removes all your data.

Third-party services

  • Google/YouTube APIs — for chat and channel data
  • Kick API — for chat and channel data
  • Discord — announcements, if you connect a server
  • Vercel — hosting and serverless functions
  • E-mail provider (Gmail) — delivers support requests to our mailbox
  • Upstash — data storage (Redis)

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts. OmniChat's own activity log is described above.

Your rights

You can revoke access anytime:

  • In OmniChat: Settings → Disconnect account
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Kick: Settings → Authorized Applications

Contact
